We built MedFair because no one should need a law degree to read a hospital bill.

MedFair is an AI-powered advocate for patients navigating America's most confusing paperwork: medical bills, insurance denials, and medical debt collection.

Our Mission

Give every American the tools to fight back.

Medical billing in the United States is built on complexity that overwhelmingly favors hospitals, insurers, and debt collectors. Industry research has repeatedly found that a large share of itemized medical bills contain errors, duplicate charges, or incorrect codes — yet most patients simply pay, because deciphering a bill or writing a formal appeal feels impossible without specialized knowledge.

MedFair exists to close that knowledge gap instantly. We built an AI system trained on billing codes, payer denial language, and consumer debt-collection law so that any patient — regardless of income, education, or legal access — can understand exactly what happened to their bill and what to do next.

Domain Expertise

Our AI models are grounded in real CPT/HCPCS billing codes, standard EOB denial reason codes, and the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA) — the same frameworks medical billing advocates use professionally.

We built MedFair around a strict "Ephemeral Processing" rule: your documents are analyzed in memory in real time and instantly deleted. We hold zero long-term database storage of medical records.
